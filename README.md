## 🚀 About Me

I'm a self-taught **Full-Stack Developer** and an **Agricultural Economics** student at **Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University**. I build at the intersection of **data, AI and agriculture**, combining on-the-ground domain knowledge with software that actually ships.

My goal is to grow into a **Data Scientist** and do **Agricultural AI research**, building tools that help farmers and agri-researchers make better decisions.
